I'm a 4th year Computer Science PhD Candidate at the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) advised by Professors [Hari Sundaram](https://sundaram.cs.illinois.edu/) and [Tal August](https://talaugust.github.io/). 

I'm broadly interested in *AI for social good*. **I study how AI systems can broaden access to justice.** I pursue this goal by (1) investigating how people use AI systems for access to justice, (2) creating novel interactions that augment people's ability to gain access to justice, and (3) evaluating AI systems' ability to broaden access to justice. 

Before joining UIUC, I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering and a Bachelor’s degree in Neurobiology from [University of California San Diego](https://www.ucsd.edu/). I was fortunate to work with Professors [Stephen MacNeil](https://stevemacn.github.io/) and [Steven Dow](https://spdow.ucsd.edu/about/) on Transparent and Controllable AI memory management, Design Thinking, and Education.
